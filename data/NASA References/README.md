---
Reference documents about fire safety in crewed spaceflight, downloaded from NASA.

- Combustion Science - A Researcher's Guide (ISS): NASA guide to combustion research on the International Space Station: why low gravity matters, past research, lessons learned and future opportunities. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/a-researchers-guide-to-combustion-science/
- Apollo 1 - Senate Report: U.S. Senate report on the 1967 Apollo 204 cabin fire, which happened in a 100% oxygen atmosphere during a launch pad test. Source: https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-1
- Apollo 13 - Review Board Report and Mission Report: investigation and mission report of the 1970 oxygen tank explosion (an oxygen-system failure, not a cabin fire). Source: https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-13
- Mir fire (1997): NASA History article, no formal report linked. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/history/25-years-ago-fire-aboard-space-station-mir/
